A first-person view of a whimsical earthen cottage built directly into a grassy hillside. Uneven stone steps cross the foreground through thick lawn, leafy plants, and clusters of purple and pink flowers. A large round green wooden door sits beneath a broad brick arch in the middle ground, flanked by two circular divided windows and curved timber supports. Ivy and dense shrubs cover much of the plaster facade and turf roof, while mature branches spread overhead. A bright orange pumpkin rests at the left edge, and damp greenery, weathered wood, masonry, and soft daylight give the dwelling a secluded rural character.
